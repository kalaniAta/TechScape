## 1. Salary Disclosure Transparency (RQ6)
- **Overall Disclosure Rate:** 27.7% (83 of 300 postings disclosed numeric compensation).
- **Undisclosed Proportion:** 72.3% (217 postings) listed qualitative terms (*Negotiable*, *Market Standard*).
- **Category with Highest Disclosure:** `QA & Test Automation` (33.9% disclosed).
