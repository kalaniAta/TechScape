1. Dual-Stack Fluency for Undergraduates

Evidence (Empirical Data): 35% of all job openings require Java, C# .NET, or Python, coupled with modern containerization (Docker, Kubernetes) and Git.

Guidance: Focus on mastering one core backend OOP language deeply while building real projects with Docker and GitHub Actions.

Academic Evidence Base
🚀

2. Explore Beyond Pure Software Engineering

Evidence (Empirical Data): Over 45% of total advertised hiring demand exists in Cloud/DevOps, Data/AI, QA Automation, and Security.

Guidance: Specializing early in Cloud Infrastructure (AWS/Terraform) or Test Automation (Selenium/Playwright) offers less crowded entry pathways.

Academic Evidence Base
💼

3. Target Structured Associate & Internship Tracks

Evidence (Empirical Data): Over 25% of advertised postings accept ≤ 1 year experience, with standard intern stipends (LKR 40,000–50,000) and starting associate salaries (LKR 110,000–160,000).

Guidance: Early internships significantly accelerate placement into mid-level tiers where compensation scales up to LKR 350,000–500,000+.
