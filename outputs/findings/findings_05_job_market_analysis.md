## 1. Volume & Temporal Distribution (RQ1)

## 2. Career Category Distribution (RQ2)
- **Dominant Category:** `Software Engineering` accounts for the largest share at 42.7% (128 postings).
- **Secondary Tracks:** `QA & Test Automation` (20.7%) and `Cloud & DevOps` (11.3%).

## 3. Entry-Level Accessibility (RQ4)
- **Overall Entry-Level Proportion:** 32.7% of synthetic positions (98 of 300) require <= 1 year of experience.
- **Highest Entry-Level Share:** `Cyber Security` (50.0% entry-level).
- **Lowest Entry-Level Share:** `IT Systems & Infrastructure` (25.0% entry-level).
